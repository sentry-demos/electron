About us

Empower Plant is an IoT company determined to keep house plants happy. After reading Michael Pollan's 2013 New Yorker article ("The Intelligent Plant"), the wife-and-wife founding team invested their life savings in measuring and improving the emotional state of their leafy housemates. Seven years later, the company's grown from its humble roots in the couples' backyard greenhouse into a Series C-funded San Francisco startup and the world's most accurate plant mood measurer.

Today, the company employs 170 people (and 852 plants) in San Francisco, New York City, Denver, and Kiev.
